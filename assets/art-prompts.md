# 封面插画生成记录

使用内置 ImageGen 生成，三张原图随项目保存在此目录。没有使用远程图片链接。

共用风格提示：

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: square vinyl record album cover illustration for a beautifully understated daily rhythm web app. Style: elegant midcentury modern editorial gouache and risograph print on textured paper, simplified poetic shapes, fine analog grain, sophisticated minimal composition, full bleed square artwork. No letters, no typography, no logos, no vinyl disc, no borders, no watermark. This is one of a cohesive three-cover series.

- `morning.png`：Sunrise over softly rolling Tuscan hills, a warm apricot sun emerging behind terracotta and golden ochre hills, tiny cypress silhouettes, pale butter yellow sky. Warm hopeful morning light.
- `afternoon.png`：A still life of a clear glass of iced tea and a small pear on a pale sage green table beside a sunlit window, soft leafy shadows, distant green hills, restful warm afternoon. Muted sage, moss and cream.
- `night.png`：A tranquil indigo blue sea under a small crescent moon and a few delicate stars, dark blue rolling cliffs and a warmly glowing tiny window on the shore. Soft periwinkle, midnight navy and warm cream.
